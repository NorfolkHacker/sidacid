FINDING: JACK xruns under PipeWire are not reported
- pw-top showed ERR 31 on cpal_client_out under a forced 32-frame quantum + CPU load,
  but the app's ⚠ did not count them. A probe (cpal JACK stream burning 20 ms per
  ~10.7 ms callback) printed no error-callback events at all: PipeWire's libjack does
  not deliver xrun callbacks to cpal clients. The xrun path (is_xrun → counter) only
  fires under a real jackd. Our own render-overrun counter (Status::overruns) still
  catches overruns SidAcid causes. No code change; documented here.
